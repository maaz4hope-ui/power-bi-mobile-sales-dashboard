# Mobile Sales Dashboard — Power BI

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing mobile phone sales performance across India, covering revenue, quantity, payment methods, and customer satisfaction.

📊 **Tools:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query

---

## 📌 Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | ₹769M |
| Total Quantity Sold | 19K units |
| Transactions | 4K |
| Average Sale Value | ₹40K |

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Delhi leads sales among all cities**, generating ₹20.3 Cr, followed by Mumbai (₹12.7 Cr) and Ranchi (₹3.1 Cr)
- **Apple and Samsung are the top-performing brands**, each generating over ₹16 Cr in sales with similar transaction volumes (~780 transactions each)
- **UPI is the most-used payment method** (26.4% of transactions), closely followed by Debit Card, Credit Card, and Cash — payment preference is fairly evenly split
- **Customer satisfaction skews positive**: 1,488 five-star ratings vs only 309 one-star ratings, suggesting strong overall customer experience
- **Sales show mild seasonality**, with July as the peak month (1,700 units) and February the lowest (1,451 units)
- **Weekend sales (Friday–Saturday) outperform most weekdays**, peaking at ₹11.46 Cr on Friday

---

## ⚙️ Dashboard Features

- City-wise sales map of India
- Monthly quantity trend line
- Payment method breakdown (donut chart)
- Customer ratings funnel
- Top-selling mobile models (bar chart)
- Day-of-week sales trend
- Brand-wise summary table
- Interactive slicers: Month, Mobile Model, Payment Method, Brand, Day Name
